I am an **Electrical and Computer Engineering** student at **The University of Texas at Austin** (minor in **Semiconductor Science**, graduating May 2028), specializing in the **Integrated Circuits (IC) track**. My work sits at the intersection of VLSI design, computer architecture, post-silicon validation, and high-speed hardware.

## 🔭 What I'm Working On Now

*  **Performance Modeling Intern @ Granular Labs**: Co-authoring a research paper on **PackageSim**, a package-aware thermal-architectural simulator for chiplet-based systems. I correlate performance-model output (e.g., gem5) with package thermal imaging for custom HBM, and evaluate how packaging (CoWoS-L, CoWoS-S, monolithic) shifts peak temperature and throughput under identical LLM workloads.
*  **Undergraduate Researcher @ [Akinwande Nano Research Group](https://github.com/ArjunChati/MATLAB-Circuit-Synthesis)**: Building an automated pipeline that turns measured 0.5–110 GHz S-parameters into causal, passive, SPICE-ready RLC macromodels (vector fitting in MATLAB) for wearable 2D X-phene RF sensors, verified in Cadence Virtuoso and Synopsys HSPICE.
*  **Founding Chair, IEEE Solid-State Circuits Society @ UT**: Starting UT's SSCS chapter to grow the circuits community on campus.

## 🛠️ Featured Projects

###  Silicon & Architecture
*  **16-bit ALU in 45 nm Standard Cells** (Oct 2026): A 22-operation Kogge-Stone ALU built only from NAND2/NAND3/AOI21/INV cells in Cadence Virtuoso/Innovus and Synopsys PrimeTime. Cut post-APR delay **41%** (1.20 → 0.70 ns) through a radix-3 prefix tree and re-floorplanning; verified against a Python golden model across 720,896 vectors with 0 errors.
*  **[CPU Cycle-Accurate Model](https://github.com/ArjunChati/CPUcycleaccurateModel)** and **[Prefetcher Performance Correlation](https://github.com/ArjunChati/Prefetcher-Performance-Correlation)**: Ongoing performance-modeling experiments in C/Python.
*  **[Custom 32-bit RISC Processor](https://github.com/ArjunChati/Custom-32bit-RISC-Processor)**: An RTL implementation in Verilog featuring a multi-cycle FSM, custom ISA, and a 27-opcode ALU.

###  Hardware & RF
*  **[RF-to-SPICE Modeling Pipeline](https://github.com/ArjunChati/MATLAB-Circuit-Synthesis)**: MATLAB toolchain that synthesizes a "virtual human wrist" RLC network from measured tissue S-parameters for co-simulation with wearable antennas.
*  **Intel N100 Carrier Board**: A high-speed x86 carrier board designed in KiCad, routing USB 3.0 (90 Ω), HDMI and Ethernet (100 Ω) differential pairs on a custom stack-up, brought up through to boot.
